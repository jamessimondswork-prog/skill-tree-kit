# How the learner likes to learn

Read before building or adapting any topic. Learner preferences only; skill-building rules live in `.claude/skills/learn/lessons.md`. Setup fills in the blanks from `skill-tree/setup.json`. Update a line when they tell you something new, with the date.

- **Name:** (from setup)
- **Job:** (from setup, one sentence, general terms only)
- **Time:** (from setup, e.g. "about 2 hours a week")
- **Learns best by:** (from setup: videos, reading or doing)
- **Interests:** (from setup; picks the project data)
- **Focus:** about 30 minutes at a time unless they say otherwise. Pick up and drop easily.
- **Level:** treat every new topic as a complete beginner until placement or records say otherwise. Define every term. Example first.
- **Game feel:** XP, ranks, trophies with story, NPCs, streak fire. Skill level must reflect real ability (Rank Trials), not effort.
- **Everything in the page:** no pasting work into chat. Asking Claude for help is optional.
- **Device:** everything must work at phone width.
- **Placement:** offered on every role topic, easy to skip.
- **Communities:** show 1-3 well-run ones per topic, never pushed. Opt-outs: none yet.
- **Data:** public or made-up only. Nothing from the work project.
- **Honesty:** never claim learning they haven't done. The Skill Tree is Claude's build, not theirs.
